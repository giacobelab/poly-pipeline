# Code of Conduct

PolyCI builds training labs used by students and instructors. Everyone who
takes part here (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) is expected to
help keep it a place people are glad to learn and contribute in.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Linux, CI
  or open source. Questions are welcome.
- Keep feedback about the work, and make it specific and constructive.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect the lab authors' decisions about their own labs.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or discriminatory attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting lab solutions, level passwords or walkthroughs in issues or pull
  requests. The labs are graded coursework for some of their users.
- Using the project to attack systems you do not own or are not authorized to
  test.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block
people who do not follow this code, in proportion to what happened.

To report a problem privately, email mruff[at]psu[.]edu. Reports are read
only by the maintainer and kept confidential.

This code applies in all PolyCI project spaces and when someone represents the
project elsewhere.
